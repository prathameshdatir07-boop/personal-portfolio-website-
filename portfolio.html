About Me & Philosophy

Inclusive design as an architectural foundation, not an afterthought.

Semantic Purity

Harnessing native HTML5 landmark elements to provide inherent assistive tech mappings, reducing custom ARIA over-engineering wherever native controls suffice.

Audited Contrast

All text, buttons, and interaction borders exceed the 4.5:1 ratio for regular text and 7:1 for enhanced AAA certification across both light and dark perspectives.

Keyboard Efficiency

Fully operational without mouse dependency. Logical tab indices, explicit focus rings, and screen-reader status indicators maintain high user autonomy.
